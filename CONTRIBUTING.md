# Contributing

Contributions are welcome — this blueprint is meant to be stress-tested and improved in public.

## Contributor License Grant

By submitting a contribution (pull request, patch, issue content, or document edit) you:

1. Certify you have the right to submit it (Developer Certificate of Origin, v1.1 —
   https://developercertificate.org).
2. License your contribution under this repository's applicable layer license
   (docs → CC BY-SA 4.0; code → AGPL-3.0-or-later), **and additionally** grant
   NUMEN Labs LLC a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use,
   modify, sublicense, and **re-license** your contribution — including under commercial
   licenses — so the project's dual-licensing model remains possible.
3. Retain your own copyright. You are credited in git history; substantial contributors are
   credited in the docs.

If your employer owns your work product, ensure you have permission before contributing.

## What we most need

Technical validation (structural, fire, hydraulics), pilot-standard definitions from
practitioners, blade-supply introductions, and corrections to any number in the Data Dossier —
every figure carries its source, so bring yours.
